Welcome! I am Yameng Fan (范雅萌), a sixth-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Hong Kong. Before joining HKU, I received my M.S. and B.S. in Economics from Beijing Normal University.

My research interests include macro development, urban economics, and the Chinese economy. My current research examines how market-based land policies shape rural-to-urban migration, agricultural production, and urban expansion.
