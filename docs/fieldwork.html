Field Visits

Fieldwork that informs my research on land institutions, migration, and urban development in China.

Chongqing Land Coupon Reform

I visited the Chongqing Country Land Exchange, where land coupons are traded, and three rural villages that supplied coupons, where I interviewed village officials about the reclamation of rural construction land.

Related paper: Structural Change and Food Security Constraints: A Market Solution

Rural Surveys in Henan, Hunan, and Yunnan

Together with faculty and fellow students, I conducted field surveys of rural households in Henan, Hunan, and Yunnan.

Related paper: The Farm Problem: A Quantitative Analysis

Urban Renewal in Shenzhen

I interviewed practitioners at an urban renewal company and visited urban villages (chengzhongcun) undergoing redevelopment in Shenzhen.

Related project: Neighborhood Fit and Spatial Spillovers from Urban Renewal
