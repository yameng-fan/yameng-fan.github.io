Field Visits

Visits to villages and urban development sites in China have helped shape my research questions and deepen my understanding of the institutions I study. This page shares selected photographs and observations from these visits.

Chongqing Land Coupon Reform

I visited the Chongqing Country Land Exchange, where land coupons are traded, and three rural villages that supplied coupons, where I interviewed village officials about the reclamation of rural construction land.

Related paper: Structural Change and Food Security Constraints: A Market Solution

Field Visits in Henan, Hunan, and Yunnan

Together with Professor Xiaodong Zhu, Qinghua Shi, Qingen Gai and fellow students, I visited villages in Henan, Hunan, and Yunnan that participate in China’s national rural fixed-point observation program. The visits helped deepen our understanding of agricultural production and rural livelihoods and informed our research on farmers’ welfare.

Related paper: The Farm Problem: A Quantitative Analysis

Urban Renewal in Shenzhen

I interviewed practitioners at an urban renewal company and visited urban villages (chengzhongcun) undergoing redevelopment in Shenzhen.

Related project: Neighborhood Fit and Spatial Spillovers from Urban Renewal
