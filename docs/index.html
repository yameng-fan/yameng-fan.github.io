Welcome! I am Yameng Fan (范雅萌), a fifth-year Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at the University of Hong Kong.

My research interests include macro development, spatial economics, and the Chinese economy. My current research examines how market-based land policies shape rural-to-urban migration, agricultural production, and urban expansion.
